# Job Autopilot Filler — Install Instructions

## One-time setup (takes 2 minutes)

1. Open Chrome and go to: chrome://extensions
2. Turn on "Developer mode" (toggle in top right)
3. Click "Load unpacked"
4. Navigate to: /Users/em/job-autopilot/chrome-extension
5. Click "Select Folder"
6. The 🦄 icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar

## Every time you use it

1. Make sure your job autopilot is running at localhost:3001
2. Click the 🦄 icon in your toolbar
3. Click "Sync Profile" — this loads your info from the autopilot
4. Go to a job application page
5. Paste your cover letter into the box (copy from your Applications tab)
6. Click "Fill This Application"
7. Review what was filled and submit!

## Works on
- Workday (myworkdayjobs.com)
- iCIMS
- Taleo
- Greenhouse
- Lever
- Ashby
- SmartRecruiters
- BambooHR
- Jobvite
- And most other job application forms

## Tips
- Always review before hitting Submit — some fields may need manual adjustment
- For Workday: wait for the page to fully load before clicking Fill
- Cover letter: copy it from your Applications tab each time for the right tailored version
- If a field doesn't fill, you can always type it manually — the extension handles the bulk of it
